# General mathematical prize research

Research commissioned by Ryan Wright and conducted with ChatGPT and AI research agents.

## Status — 2026-10-09

**No prize has been won or claimed. No sponsor has been contacted.**

This repository records original-source prize checks, mathematical arguments, reproducible experiments, failed approaches, and continuation checkpoints.

The first selected target is Jeffrey Shallit's £50 Problem 9: determine whether all shifted Hankel determinants of the fixed point of `1 -> 12, 2 -> 23, 3 -> 14, 4 -> 32` are nonzero. One exact singular matrix would be a complete counterexample. The general problem remains unresolved in this project.

The initial audit and experimental results are being prepared. Every result must distinguish a finite computation, a limited theorem, and a complete solution. Sponsor contact, prize claims, and external submissions require Ryan's approval. Substantive AI involvement must be disclosed.

Primary sources: [current sponsor updates](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/talks.html), [original statements, slides 32–35](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/Talks/bc4.pdf). [Prize Problem Ledger](https://prizeproblems.org/) is a discovery index, not a payment guarantee.
