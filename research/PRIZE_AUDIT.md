# Mathematical cash-prize audit

Audit date: **2026-10-09 UTC**. Research and code were generated with ChatGPT and AI research agents at Ryan's request. **No complete prize solution, payment, sponsor contact, submission, or private eligibility confirmation resulted.**

## Decision

We began with [Prize Problem Ledger](https://prizeproblems.org/), then checked original offers, sponsor updates, recent papers, and additional AI-oriented programs. The result is **20 ranked conditional cash research candidates**, not 20 prizes confirmed to satisfy every preference. No inspected active offer in the preferred amount range simultaneously supplied an explicit fully-AI cash eligibility promise and all the other preferred conditions.

Silence about AI is an unresolved term, not permission or prohibition. Likewise, an offer still visible on a sponsor's page does not prove that no unpublished claimant exists. We did not contact sponsors because that requires Ryan's approval. There is no reason to seek claim approval at this checkpoint: neither research target has been solved.

The initial selection was **Shallit #9**, whose negative answer could be certified by one singular integer matrix. After a substantial search and an all-shifts bounded-order theorem, the best next allocation is **Sun's $90 identity**, renewed publicly in May 2026. Its exact analytic reductions are now available. This final ranking incorporates the session's research results; it is a qualitative allocation judgment, not estimated probabilities of winning.

## Reading the ranking

- **Renewed:** the sponsor restated the conjecture/offer in 2026.
- **Posted:** the original offer remains accessible, but current payout and priority need confirmation.
- **No journal clause found:** none appears in the inspected offer; this is not an affirmative sponsor waiver.
- Every main-table row has **AI eligibility unconfirmed**. No professional credential or entry fee was stated in the inspected personal offers. All can be researched without buying specialized hardware; several remain mathematically difficult and unattractive for a quick win.
- Currency amounts are kept in the sponsor's currency. No exchange-rate conversion or financial guarantee is implied.
- The lower-ranked problems are backups. Their inclusion records legitimate mathematical cash offers and a plausible proof/certificate route, not a claim that famous open problems have become easy.

## What research actually happened

See [Shallit #9](shallit9.md), [Sun $90](sun90.md), the [integral derivation](sun90_integral.md), and [continuation literature](sun90_literature.md). None is a complete prize solution. The Shallit result is independently checked computer-assisted nonvanishing through order 2,049 at every shift. The Sun result is a proved formal differential equation plus analytic reductions, with its constant evaluation open.

Before any future prize claim, Ryan must approve sponsor contact/submission and the sponsor must resolve AI eligibility, continued availability, authorship conditions, and review/payment requirements. A concrete solution and reviewable disclosure should be prepared first. This checkpoint does not initiate that process or promise background work after the session ends.
